    Common questions.

    Something else? Ask on GitHub.

    Is Solrino free?

    Yes, it's free to download and use, with no ads.

    What does it run on?

    Windows. There isn't a Mac or Linux version.

    Why does Windows show a warning when I install it?

    The app isn't signed yet, so SmartScreen doesn't recognize it. Click More info, then Run anyway.

    Will the on-screen pings work over my game?

    In borderless and windowed games, yes. Exclusive fullscreen can hide them. The cards are a normal always-on-top window that clicks pass through, and they don't touch the game itself. Every game's anti-cheat has its own rules, so check them if you're unsure.

    Can I keep my Chatterino or Moltorino setup?

    Yes. The first time you run setup, it asks if you'd like to bring over your settings, chat tabs and accounts from another fork.

    How do I get a badge?

    Solrino's owner gives out most badges, and event badges are free for everyone. Once you have one, type /vanity in chat and equip it.

    Who can see my badge?

    Everyone using Solrino, usually within a couple of seconds of it changing. People on the Twitch website or other chat apps won't see it.

    Download Solrino.
