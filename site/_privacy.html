Privacy Policy

Last updated November 5, 2026

This explains what data Solrino uses, where it goes, and how to control it. Solrino doesn't sell your data, show ads, or use analytics to track you.

On your computer

Your settings, tabs, saved messages and logs stay on your PC, in %APPDATA%\Solrino. Your Twitch login token is saved in that folder too, in an ordinary settings file, so never share that folder or its files with anyone. Solrino can keep other logins in Windows Credential Manager. Uninstalling Solrino keeps this folder so a reinstall picks up your settings, so delete it yourself if you want everything gone.

Solrino's badge service

Solrino runs a small service on Cloudflare so everyone using the app can see badges.

  • What it stores. Your Twitch user ID, the badges you've been given, the one you've equipped, and the order and visibility you chose for your badges. This list is public, because every copy of Solrino reads it to show badges. The service stores nothing about you until you get a badge or save your badge setup.
  • Your Twitch login. When you save your badges, your app sends your Twitch login token to the service, which passes it to Twitch to confirm who you are. Solrino's code uses it only for that check, and doesn't store or log it.
  • Live updates. Your app keeps a connection open to the service so badge changes appear within a few seconds.

To have your badge data removed, ask through Solrino's GitHub page. Cloudflare handles this traffic, and may keep its own technical logs under its privacy policy.

Discord

If Discord is open on your PC, Solrino can show your activity there, including the chat you're in and whether you're modding. It talks to the Discord app on your computer, and your Discord friends can see this. You can turn it off, or hide the chat name, in the General section of Settings. Discord's privacy policy applies.

Other services the app connects to

Like other chat apps, Solrino connects directly to other services. They can see your IP address and whatever the feature sends them, and their own privacy policies apply.

  • Chat platforms. Twitch, plus Kick, YouTube or TikTok if you use them.
  • Emotes and badges. Services such as 7TV, BetterTTV and FrankerFaceZ, plus public badge lists including Moltorino's. Solrino only reads from these lists.
  • Message history. Recent-message services fill in chat when you join a channel.
  • Features you choose to use. Translation, link previews, image uploads, settings backup sharing, and signing in to Moltorino features go to the services that provide them. That includes Moltorino, which runs separately from Solrino.
  • Updates. Solrino checks GitHub for new versions and downloads them from there.

This website

This site doesn't use cookies, analytics or ad trackers. It loads the latest version information from GitHub, and it's hosted on Cloudflare, so both can see your IP address when you visit. The chat on the home page is a demo: what you type there stays in your browser.

Children

Solrino isn't meant for children under 13, or under the minimum age for Twitch where you live.
