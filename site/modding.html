Change the game in one click.

Your most used games sit by the chat box as small buttons, and the ones you use most move to the front. Click one and it sends !game fortnite.

A bot in the channel has to support the command. If a channel uses a different command, set it in the game popup, and Solrino saves it for that channel. The bar's position, size and alignment are in settings.
