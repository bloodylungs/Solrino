How it works.

  1. 1

    Get a badge

    Most badges come from Solrino's owner and can't be claimed or bought. Event badges are free for everyone.

  2. 2

    Equip it

    Type /vanity in chat, pick your badge and save.

  3. 3

    Everyone sees it

    It appears next to your name for everyone using Solrino, with no restart.

Badges only show inside Solrino. People on the Twitch website or other chat apps won't see them.
