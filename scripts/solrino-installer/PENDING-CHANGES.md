# Changes since v1.0.43

Not released yet. Collect everything here, then use it for the release notes.

## App

### New

- Now playing overlay. Shows the song playing in Spotify on top of your screen, even over games, with album art, song name, previous, pause and next buttons and a progress bar. Place it anywhere on any monitor. It has its own Now Playing settings tab with a live preview.
- Now playing themes (Spotify, Apple Music, Minimal, Neon) and looks: glass, solid or transparent, a one-line layout, rounded or square corners, more colours including one from the album art, a glowing border, size and see-through choices.
- Now playing album art that spins like a record or glows with the music, bars that bounce with the music, and a slide-in when the song changes. It can also pop up for 8 seconds only when the song changes.
- Now playing times by the progress bar, long song names that slide along, shuffle and repeat buttons, and scroll over it to change Spotify's volume.
- /song command. Sends the song you're listening to in chat, like "Now playing: Song by Artist".
- Reactions. Hover a message and click the smiley button to react with any emoji or emote; other Solrino users in that chat see it.
- First time chatter line above someone's first ever message in a channel, like Twitch.
- StreamNook badges in chat, with their own spot in Vanity's badge order.
- Badge limit. Past the number you choose, extra badges become a +N you can hover to see.
- Chat spoofer, an experimental setting that sends your messages the way the Twitch website does, so they may count toward your Twitch Recap. It's off by default and needs a Twitch web login in Authentication settings.
- /cheer command. It copies your cheer and opens the channel's Twitch chat, ready to send.
- Auto-open for live channels. In Settings > Notifications, pick Twitch channels and Solrino opens a tab for each one as soon as it goes live, so you never miss a stream.
- Mute Tab. Right-click a tab to stop its sounds, flashing, unread marks and ping popups.
- Pick from open tabs button for live notifications, to tick channels from your open tabs and folders.
- Unban request alerts. Streamers and mods see each new request in chat, with the person's message and a link to the inbox.
- Right-click menu on 7TV emotes to add them to, or remove them from, your channel's 7TV emotes, for streamers and mods connected to 7TV in Vanity.
- Recent button in the stream info editor, to reuse titles you've used in that channel before.
- Ping popup options. Choose its colour, how long it stays, and whether it slides or fades.
- Compact chat input, mod button spacing, and a custom colour for the space around tabs.
- Skip button on the update popup, and a setting to turn update popups off.

### Changed

- The timeout mod buttons in chat are now stopwatch icons that match the ban, delete and pin icons, with the length on one line next to them, like "5m". Hover one to see "Timeout for 5 minutes".
- The emote carousel is on by default, so cycling emotes with Tab shows their pictures.

### Fixed

- A crash in Vanity. Clicking a badge in the Moltorino Badges tab could close Solrino.
- Missing a newer update. If you chose Update later and another version came out before you quit, Solrino now gets the newest one.
- The game bar forgetting which games you use most. It now saves right away instead of only when Solrino closes.
- Game bar buttons moving under your mouse. The order now updates once you move the mouse away, so a quick second click can't hit the wrong game.
- Solrino slowly holding on to old raid and First records. Old ones are now cleared out while it runs.
