# Changes since v1.0.31

Not released yet. Collect everything here, then use it for the release notes.

## App

- Your most used predictions now start with one click. Clicking one starts it right away with its saved options and length, no extra steps.
- New Seasonal theme. It changes its colors with the time of year: autumn browns and orange, winter ice blue, spring greens and pink, and summer teal and yellow. If the app is open when the season changes, it switches by itself within an hour.

## Website

- New look. The top of the home page now shows a game with the Solrino chat window next to it, and ping cards land on the game like they do in the app.
- Cards and panels are solid instead of see-through glass, and the moving light blobs, dot grid and fancy italic heading words are gone.
- The FAQ sits next to its heading, with a link to ask on GitHub.
- Not deployed yet. Run `python _build.py` then `npx wrangler deploy` from `site/` at release time.
